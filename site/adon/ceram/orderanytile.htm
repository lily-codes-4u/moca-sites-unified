Don Archer digital ceramic art

Don Archer is one of the web's most renowned and successful digital artists. Now almost his entire repetoire of digital art as presented on his website is made available as ceramic tile art on a custom basis. These include some of his classic images, his fractal zoo images, his raytraced fractals, and his photographs, most notably his series of NYC bridges. All of these images are now available in high quality ceramics, authoritatively and colorfully reproduced.
