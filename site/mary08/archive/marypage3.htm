"Sub88:Understair" by David Vineïs

Is it fine art or a backdrop for advertising? Perhaps it would be best presented without the white letter-like boxed imagery and wording on the right. I do like the play of light and the use of grays with a strong complimentary neon green. These elements along with an artful overall composition make it notable.

Ahead of the Wave

The contemporary art collector has yet to fully recognize the value of digital fine arts. In part it is a lack of understanding for which we are all tasked to address. We might start with better understanding why collectors buy what they buy. It is most certainly insight worthy of consideration and investigation.

:::Recommended Reading:::
Books to encourage a better understanding of the art market from a collectors perspective:

In English:

Owning Art, The Contemporary Art Collector's Handbook;Editions of "Triumph of painting of the Saatchi gallery,(three volumes) + Abstract America, Germania and USA Today; “Seven Days in the Art World” by Sarah Thornton; "Women Gallerists"(ISBN is 978-3-7757-1975-9).

In German:

“Artinvestor Handbuch” edited by Edgar Quadt; “Frauen, die handeln. Galeristinnen in 20. und 21. Jahrhundert”,by Claudia Herstatt

In Italian:

“Economia delle Arti” by Candela and Scorcu;Angela Vettese's "Capire l’arte contemporanea" and "Ma questo è un quadro?"; "Come comprare l’arte contemporanea" by L. Buck and J. Greer.

Matchsticks….pins…? Okay it is what it is and I like it! Visually stimulating, I can easily see this artwork printed in a large scale format hanging on my living room wall. Nicely executed the imagery keeps your eyes moving resting only momentarily on varying depths of detail. Emotion runs deep within the work's simplicity. I am particularly drawn to the right side of the image where background, mid-ground and foreground blend into a light-headed frenzy. Frankly, it’s just cool.
