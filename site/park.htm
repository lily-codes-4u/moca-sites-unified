Caren K. Park

Caren K. Park

fractal art

Biographical notes
Caren Park is a prolific and talented fractal artist who has made hundreds of her images available to viewers via CompuServe's Graph Development forum. She is a resident of Issaquah, Washington, and worked in computer support, "a job," she noted at the time , "that does not let me scream primally often enough (scares the VP's)..." She now owns and operates a bookstore in Issaquah. She is herself a heavy reader, listens to music with an "eclectic ear," and used to compete as a semi-professional bowler. She bought with her partner a "huge" house some time ago and says that she was adopted by two cats.

Commentary
Caren Park generates classical fractal art, at which she is an adept, working with facility in almost all fractal types. There does not seem to be a striving for effect in Park's work. Her talent is instinctive: beautifully composed, often serene, brilliantly colored. As a musician may have perfect pitch, so Park has a perfect eye, and often works from her own color palettes. The image shown here, called "Snakeskin Starfish" draws on a palette carefully shaded in tones of gold, red and brown. She writes that this image "jumped out at me after lots of tinkering," and that she "was reminded of a 4-legged starfish near/at a spectacular sunset..."
