Pat Lichty

Pat Lichty

computer-drawn and -painted art

Biographical notes
Pat Lichty was born in 1962, and is one of three artists in his family He gives his home as North Canton, Ohio, and Minneapolis, MN. After several years as an electrical engineer, Lichty completed art studies in 1989 and pursued theoretical and philosophic issues in the visual arts. Besides his digital art, Lichty is a sculpturist, and several of his works in neon are in permanent gallery collections in New York and San Francisco. He has won over a dozen regional awards for his sculptural and digital work.

Commentary
If Lichty's art appeared on canvas, it would be difficult to believe that they were computer created. In this sense, Lichty is a mainstream artist, albeit very progressive. He works in an expressionistic mode, and has an endearing, almost free-hand way of communicating. To the casual viewer his work my seem inaccessible, but a closer look suggests that a major artist may be at work. "Coll2" is a self-portrait, based on photo scans, its interest a consequence of overlays. "Coll3" is subtitled "Improvisation 1", a play of form and color. "Coll5" he calls "Black Spot". Lichty uses multiple paint and draw programs, including CorelDRAW!, Fractal Painter and Aldus Photostyler, among others.
