Yes, you can create your own fractal by a simple input of random numbers and functions and see your image displayed on this site with your name on it. You don't need to know any math. It's a collaboration between us and it's free. For me it's a labor of love.

I will draw a 256-color fractal for you in Fractint, the comprehensive freeware program in which all my fractals are drawn. You won't know what your fractal will look like until you see it, but neither do I. No fractal artist knows what his image will look like until it is actually drawn on screen. You may very well surprise yourself at the beauty of your image, even though your input is random.

CREDITS
Print art included in the collection of the Ball State University Art Museum, Muncie, IN.

Work exhibited in MOCA: the Museum of Computer Art.

Some 180 fractals included in a CD-ROM, Fractal Frenzy II, Postcards from the Edge of Space, published 1995 by Walnut Creek.

Several one-man and group shows, NYC, 1994-97.

Prints included in some distinquished NYC collections.

Images included in Wirehead's CD-ROM, Virtual Media Gallery by Quantum Access, 1995.

Critic Ed McCormack wrote: "In this important series of works, Archer embarks on what just may be one of the most original and exciting explorations of pictorial space in recent decades. Indeed, fractal geometry could turn out to be as important to the art of cyberspace as the picture plane was for Cubism--a staggering concept that would place Archer squarely in the forefront of the new avant-garde."(ARTspeak, May '94)
