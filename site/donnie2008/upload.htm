Image upload page

moca home contest info contact fee images

Upload images from this page. You may submit one, two or three images. Still images in JPG format only.

Please note that upload size may not exceed 300 kilobytes for any one image. Images greater than 300KB are aborted in transmission and cannot be viewed here. You should confirm kilobyte size in Photoshop or other graphic program before uploading.

Images should be about 600 pixels wide but not over 540 pixels high. Our exhibit pages accommodate this size best, giving your image(s) a strong presence on the page.

Images appear on the contest site usually within 24 to 36 hours of receipt.

NOTE
Fees are $25.00 per image, payable upon upload. Please make payment via our fee page.

When requested for filenames and titles on the succeeding page, please attach your name to the filename to make it easier for us to associate the file with you. For instance, FireEngine.jpg might be sent as DoeJohn_FireEngine.jpg.

Use browse button to find image files on your computer.
