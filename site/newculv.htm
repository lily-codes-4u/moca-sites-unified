Laura Culver

Laura Culver

cartoon art

SLIP OF THE TONGUE

Laura Culver's imagination is radical, subversively comic and politically incorrect, but it is also fierce and fearless. She is a 34 year-old fine arts major at the University of Florida in Tampa concentrating in electronic media. Her art makes mordant political, social and personal commentary.
