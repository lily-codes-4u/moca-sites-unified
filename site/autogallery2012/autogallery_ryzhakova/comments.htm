Nadja Ryzhakova

An enthusiastic iPad artist fascinated by how new technologies transform the way we create, I see drawing on mobile devices as a progressive form of art once again challenging the conventional notion of painting as the dominant medium of representation. Through my iPaintings, I explore the creative capacity of an iPad and aim to bring this new form of creative work to fine art, and in so doing, to broaden art horizons.
