Donnie 2023 Catalog

Order or preview Donnie 2023 Catalog (click image)

An elegant catalog of our Donnie 2023 contest and exhibit. Includes all 45 images entered in this event by 13 artists from 5 countries. 54 pages. Images published on its own page in authoritative full-color. Gloss paper. This is our 30th catalog documenting the history of digital art in our era and a valuable promotional tool for all contributing artists. Three prizewinners named, including five Honorable Mentions and four Director's Choices. Comments included by Don Archer on (AI) Artificial Intelligence art (five AI images included in this event).
