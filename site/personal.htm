MOCA's curator and director

Leslye Bloom is MOCA's curator. She is a distinguished artist, both conventional and digital, based in Virginia. She holds a PhD in art education and is a Cetified Art Therapist. She has been an art educator on the university level for many years and has won many prizes for her work. Her web page:
leslyebloom.com

Don Archer is MOCA co-founder, director and administrator. He operates MOCA out of his studio in Brooklyn and Prattsville, NY. He is largely responsible for the commentary and notes accompanying most of the older exhibits. He welcomes your comments. Write to:

hello@museumofcomputerart.org

Don also needs volunteers to help in conducting the site. Do you have HTML and webpage experience? Or curatorial, critical and/or literary skills? Write to:
hello@museumofcomputerart.org

Don is himself a serious digital artist whose work may be viewed on his personal webpage at:
