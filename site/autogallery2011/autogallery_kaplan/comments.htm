Yael Kaplan

"I work mainly in photography and painting. It is my goal to uncover the meeting point of these two medias. Structures and surface reveal themselves in private dialogs; leaning on memories and biography as a starting point to a dialog with the world. This body of work deals with eclectic elements as a projection to my inner world. It presents a temporary situation that is variable and can change. The changing light formation turns the works into organic, brawny objects or airy, transparent and gentle impressions (like an evasive memory or the soul of the object)."

Yael Kaplan was born in Israel where she lives, works and teaches. Education: Graduate Art Department, Photography, 'Hamidrasha Art College', 'Beit Berl', Israel. Awards: Recipient of Scholarship Excellence, Photography Department, 'Beit Berl' Art College. The Mayor Award Artist Excellence. The International Award for Art, Culture, Design and Science of man "Lorenzo il Magnifico". She works in various art media: photography, painting, printing and etching and has displayed more than 5 solo exhibitions and participated in over 28 group exhibitions all over the world. She has won awards in international photography competitions.
