Contest info

This contest is open for entry to any and all digital artists through January 31, 2007.

This is a competition and exhibit of quality digital art. Artists may enter and participate in the contest today.

This is our seventh annual online contest, one of the largest and most prestigious digital art contests on the web. First prize is $1000.00.

There is a $25.00 exhibit fee per image.

Upload one, two or three images.

Payment must accompany submission. All payments made via our fee page.

Images accepted through Wednesday, January 31, 2007.

Awards will be announced on or about February 15, 2007.

This is a juried event. First, second and third prize winners plus five honorable mentions will be selected.

Images published in an online MOCA gallery through December 31, 2007.
Copyright remains with the respective artist.

For most images, longest dimension should not exceed 540 pixels.
Images smaller than 480 pixels in longest dimension are usually too small to be competitive.

Maximum upload size is 200 kilobytes. Still images only in JPG format.
