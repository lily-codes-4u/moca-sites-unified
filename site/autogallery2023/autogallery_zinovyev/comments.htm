Leonard Zinovyev

I am an international visual artist, poet, and fiction writer, a polyglot, a full-time translator/interpreter in the fields of science, technology, and medicine. First put pen to paper back in 1997 and produced my first digital collage in 2006.

My digital collage (sur)realism is about being able to see through things, seeing things for what they are, and being able to fill the voids of silence. Combining found digital images, I produce fresh and unexpected insights into reality.

My previous publications include

- Eighteen text-based artworks + Artist Statement + a painstaking interview :) that appeared in Diaphanous Micro e-journal

https://diaphanouspress.com/2019/11/3-13-self-fulfilling-prosecutions-leonard-zinovyev/

- Particicpated in NewDigitalCollage.Art project in 2021.

http://www.newdigitalcollage.art/gallery/

My digital collages appeared in AXESS Magazine and MAINTENANT 15. My pattern poems were featured in wait.thisispoetry and The6Ress.
