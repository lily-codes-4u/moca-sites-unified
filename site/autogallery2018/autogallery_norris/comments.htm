Eugene Norris

Erstwhile mathematician/computer scientist Eugene Norris's passion is creating digital fine art. He is a prize-winning member of many professional art societies.

He has had a number of one-man shows: in St Petersburg, at Opera Central, at Eckerd College, and at Art on First; and two Artist of the Month shows at Sun Tan Art in St Pete Beach. His work is included in numerous group shows with The Society of Exhibiting Artists, SunTan Art, The Photographic Arts Society of St. Petersburg, in the Largo Public Library, The Pinellas Park Art Society, and at ASPEC at Eckerd College. In 2018 alone he received eleven awards and prizes, and five in 2017. He lives on a barrier island near St. Petersburg.
