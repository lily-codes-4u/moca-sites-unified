The Open Gallery · Milan Dobrojevic · Creator's Creator

(1 of 12)

Milan Dobrojevic writes, "I have spent many years creating my ideal of art, through different ways of visual art (painting, sculpting), but also through composing music, acting and video production. In the beginning 2002 year, I have started my researches of fractal worlds and I have been creating new formulas along with 3D, web design and other activities for two local marketing agencies. Finally, 2005 year, work on fractals preoccupies me, so 2006 year I have decided to devote myself and all my creativity and energy to one thing– fractals.

"Fractals have been usually classified as psychedelic art, although there are plenty of them which don'’t give us that kind of impression. In most of fractals you will find spirals, psychedelic colors and shapes, which are easy to make in this field - if you start to create them you will notice that most of people who create fractals are still at previously mentioned level. Actually there are only 10% of people who deeply get in this field and make new way, which presents much more than only psychedelic art. From 2005 to 2007 I have created over 6000 fractals with intention to technically improve and support new view on fractals.

"All fractals are made in Fractal Explorer program and 97% of (some of them in Apophysis) them have their own parameters. It proves us that, all exposed pictures in same percent, are pure fractals –not treated by other software."

Milan Dobrovejic's fractal website
