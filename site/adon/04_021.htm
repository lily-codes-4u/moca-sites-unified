February 2004          No.021

Some years ago after very deep exploring via a Mandelbrot formula, I happened onto a tiny area, buried far beyond the main body of the fractal, that harbored a spectacular display of fragments and "flags". This area was so deeply rooted in the set that if I had not recorded its location, I would never have been able to find it again. It took many hours of fractal rendering to bring it to screen, though with today's microchips the rendering time has been reduced from many hours to many minutes. I am still astonished at these flags and have no way of accounting for their existance, and I have never quite seen anything like them in other fractals. They are often rectangular in shape, striped and attached to a "flagpole." I have played with them for years, sent them off to various fractal contests, but nobody has ever thought as much of them as I have. I am revisiting these flags on behalf of the current gallery, and post-processing them to give them as much panache as possible.
