Tina Quatroni

"Tina M. Quatroni was born in Queens, New York in 1974 and currently resides in Dutchess County, New York. She is a self taught digital artist, who has created a plethora of images ranging from colorful and bright to dark and macabre. Pending on her emotions, mood and daily enviroment she will change and adapt to that moment in time. Tina creates for passion and hobby because of the love she has for the art. She will showcase her work on occasions but chooses to not place emphasis on constant showings. She prefers to not lose the passion of creativity by making it a business."

She says, "As a brush is to Dali, a mouse is to me. I love what I do. I enjoy finding new ways to do them. Photography was my first love. Then manipulating what I shot was my next passion. Now using my mouse as a brush and painting is my newest adoration. It is technology that keeps this world moving and evolving. I embrace it and see how far I can take it."
