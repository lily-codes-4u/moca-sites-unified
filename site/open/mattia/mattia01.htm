The Open Gallery · Gianluca Mattia · Circe

(1 of 5)

Gianluca Mattia was born in Bari (Italy) in 1975. He attended the Institute of Art where he graduated with a Master in Art and Architecture. He has also studied design and interior decoration professionally. After graduating, he specialized in digital painting and graphic design becoming an expert in 2D and 3D imagery. In 2003, Mattia states that he “redesigned the pin up genre in a modern style.” The series became available in 2006 with a Pin up Card Collection. Media -- digital painting.

2006 - Calendar of Suicide Girls; 2007 - Designed a full collection of “Go-Go Dolls and Guys” (for an Italian fashion agency) including pin-up images, logos, and gadgets; 2008 -Luna and Lauren Artwork winner "The World's Greatest Erotic Art of Today".
