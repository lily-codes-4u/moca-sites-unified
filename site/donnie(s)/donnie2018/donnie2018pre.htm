The Donnie 2018 Digital Art Contest and Exhibit

moca home view catalogs

This is a heads-up, an advisory. No action need be taken at this time!

This contest will open for entries Thursday, January 1, 2018. Entries are not accepted prior to this date.

It will be our 18th consecutive annual digital art contest. The Donnies are the most prestigious and influential digital art competition on the Web.

A modest fee per image required.

Maximum six entries per artist. Still images only. JPG format. Image size 1 - 2 megabytes. Images are uploaded via email attachment and posted chronologically to the contest site as received.

This contest is international in scope and open to all digital artists and photographers.

As an inducement for artists, MOCA has arranged for the Atelier Progressif Creative Space in Catskill NY for a live physical print exhibit of Winning and Director's Choice images from this contest. MOCA will print and hang these images at no charge to the artists. Click for details and full information about this live print exhibit at Atelier Progressif Creative Space.

It offers eight awards: first, second and third prizes plus five honorable mentions. Winning and Director's Choice images are given year-long exposure on the MOCA site and will be printed and hung in the Atelier Progressif Creative Space in Catskill NY March 3 - 17, 2018.

Contest judge is Steve Soper. Steve has been associated with MOCA as a graphics guru for many years and has had a long and distinguished career as a photographer, digital artist, graphic designer, and Photoshop specialist. He is responsible for publishing the Donnie catalog. He was co-judge for the legendary MOCA show at the Cork Gallery at Lincoln Center in 2004.

MOCA will publish a full-color catalog of the show. To view our many catalogs, click catalogs.

Contest will close for entries January 31, 2018, 11:59 p.m. ET.

MOCA is a non-profit educational and charitable organization. It us supported exclusively by contest fees and the contributions of artists, viewers, and friends. Thank you for visiting our site and participating in our activities.

Comments, inquiries, questions? Write:
hello@museumofcomputerart.org
Don Archer, MOCA director and contest administrator
