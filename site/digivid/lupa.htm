To play this animation, click on the letters NNY in the above image. This animation will play one time only, but the sound will continue for about 1/2 minute after the animation stops. When the sound stops, click on the lens to repeat the process.

In the opinion of MOCA curators, this animation is a stunning example of Flash technique welded to a brilliantly subversive, if gross, concept.
