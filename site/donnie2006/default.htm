Contest information

moca home upload contact fee donnie2005 view contest

Contest closes for entries on Thursday, January 5, 2006 at 11:59 PM.

This is our sixth annual online contest, and one of the largest and most prestigious digital art contests on the web, now embellished with a $1000.00 first prize.

There is a $15.00 exhibit fee

, payable upon acceptance of image. Only one image per artist permitted.

This is a juried and curated contest. Images will be screened by our curators for acceptance.

We can accept only a limited number of entries, so artists are strongly advised to make their submission as soon as possible.

Contest will close for entries on January 5, 2006, or earlier if oversubscribed.

Images published in an online MOCA gallery during the course of the contest. View Donnie2005 contest at MOCA.

Awards are expected to be announced on or about January 20, 2006.

Copyright remains with the respective artist.

Click upload to submit your art to the contest. No fee required for submission.

For most images, longest dimension should not exceed 540 pixels. Images smaller than 480 pixels in longest dimension may be deemed too small for acceptance.

Upload size may not exceed 140KB.

Comments, inquiries, questions? Write:
steve@freetransform.com
Steve Soper (based in Italy)
MOCA curator and contest administrator
