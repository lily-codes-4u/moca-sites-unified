The Donnie 2013

Opens for entries January 2, 2013 at 12:01 a.m ET.

The Donnie 2013 is our thirteenth annual digital art contest. It is the most prestigious digital art competition on the Web.

In recognition of difficult economic times and yet to encourage world-wide participation, fee has been set to only $39.00 per image. Payment may be made by PayPal, credit card or other means.

It is international in scope and open to all digital artists and photographers. Maximum six entries per artist. Still images only. JPG format. Image size 1 - 2 megabytes each. Images downloaded via our upload page and posted chronologically to the contest site as received.

It offers eight awards: first, second and third prizes plus five honorable mentions. Winning images are given year-long exposure on the MOCA site.

Contest judge is JD Jarvis, distinguished digital art critic and writer, co-author of "Going Digital: The Practice and Vision of Digital Artists".

MOCA will publish a full-color catalog of the show. To browse or order among our 17 published catalogs, click catalogs.

Contest will close for entries January 31, 2013, 11:59 p.m. ET.

MOCA is a non-profit educational institution supported by contest fees and the contributions of artists and viewers. Thank you for visiting our site and participating in our activities.

Comments, inquiries, questions? Write:
don@moca.virtual.museum
Don Archer, MOCA director and contest administrator
