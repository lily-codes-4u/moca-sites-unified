Neal Bridges

Neal Bridges

raytraced art

Biographical notes
Neal Bridges was born in Welland, Ontario, Canada, and resides in Toronto, "with a splendid view" of Lake Ontario." He is currently working privately in the development of software for handheld computers. When asked about his education, he writes forthrightly, "Most of what I know I learned from science fiction novels." Also: "I've spent my life around computers, and in late 1992 it all came together for me--the speed of the systems, the availability of excellent (free!) raytracing software, and my own creative impulses--and I was prompted to try my hand at the art." "Moo" was his very first raytrace effort.

Commentary
Photorealism often relies on interior subjects illuminated by postulated light sources casting shadow and reflection. Not so with Neal Bridges's "Moo" a whimsical and cartoon-like raytrace that proves that photorealism can survive, and even flourish, in the great outdoors, at high noon on a summer day, no less. Bridges thinks there is something Zen-like about cows, Holsteins in particular. His Holsteins are balloons, windswept on a pure green pasture against a cumulus blue sky, their body parts reduced to udder, teats and tail. Bridges's lightness of touch (pun intended) is a welcome reprieve from more ponderous art.
