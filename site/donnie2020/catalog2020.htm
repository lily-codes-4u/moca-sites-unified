Catalog of our Donnie 2020 contest and exhibit. Includes all 63 images entered in this event by 23 artists from 6 countries. 74 pages. Thirteen prizewinners named plus 7 images selected as Director's Choices. Introduction by Don Archer. Design and judging by Steve Soper.
