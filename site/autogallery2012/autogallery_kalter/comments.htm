Lidian Kalter

I am a Retoucher, and in my spare time I love to draw on the computer as well as create traditional paintings. This drawing took me about 3-4 days. I used Photoshop CS6 to paint in. No extra tutorials, everything by hand and a basic brush, using a few models as an example. But in a fact this is someone who doesn't exist. A stranger.
