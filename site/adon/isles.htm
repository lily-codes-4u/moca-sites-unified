Don Archer's Fractal Art

Isles of Barnett

Variations on a theme of isles. The working hypothesis was to isolate the image against its background, as if to create an island or a floating image. All images from a single formula adapted by the artist from a formula by Ron Barnett. 640 x 480 resolution.
