Don Archer Studio Archive

Don Archer, who founded the Museum of Computer Art in 1993, kept his work in a folder for each year. This archive shows 9,154 pictures from those folders and from his 3.5-inch disks that the museum's web pages never showed: early fractals, the Fractal Camera and Fractal Zoo, object scans, portraits, photo art and textures, and the numbered series of his later years.

Most of the pictures from 1996 to 2001 are U-DRAW fractals. Visitors to Don Archer's website typed a few words into his U-DRAW program, which drew a fractal from them; each picture was named for the person who made it, so here they are shown by set and number only.

Works the museum already shows are not repeated here: see Don Archer's collection and his AI art.
