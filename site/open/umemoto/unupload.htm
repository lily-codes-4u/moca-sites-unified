The Open Gallery

Image upload page

This gallery is open to all digital artists, beginner or experienced, amateur or professional. There is no charge for inclusion of your art.

This gallery is uncurated. That is, none of our curators will review images for acceptance here,
although images will be examined to assure that they do not violate standards
of propriety and good taste, as determined here.

You may upload up to 12 images to this gallery.

You may use this form only once. A second upload (now or in the future) will not be acknowledged. So before you upload it is important to decide what images and how many (up to 12) you want included in the Open Gallery.

Images will appear onsite usually within 24 to 48 hours of receipt.

Upload size may not exceed 300 kilobytes for any one image.
Images greater than 300KB are aborted in transmission and cannot be viewed here.
Please confirm kilobyte size in Photoshop or other graphic program before uploading.

Images should be about 600 pixels in width but not over 540 pixels in height.
While all images are subject to re-sizing, our exhibit pages accommodate this size best.
It will give your images the strongest presence on our pages.

JPG format only!

REMINDER: This form may be used one time only.
