Angelo Kerelov

DVA--Digital Visual Art. This is one way you can express yourself and share dreams, moods and visions using a huge variety of classic, modern, abstract and many other tools with no limits, no definitions, just momentum and a temporary inner interpretation of a concept, sound, idea, word, philosophy, the whole of reality...

Mixing whatever we need, from paintbrush and onion on canvas to complete multimedia solutions or the silence of the color missing, we structure the vision on the subject provoking our creativity, allowing us to introspect about our vast surreality--enjoying it, and making it public. In DVA Illustration and Conceptual Art are in the background in an essay trying to explain the consequences of an event, in an unspecified time zone framing the jiff in isolation, like a small piece of puzzle in the Universal Time. Because it’'s still nowhere, the object is independent and gives no idea of the whole panorama of reality.

This visual documentation of the moment is usually colored by the individual unintentional acceptance and artistic reaction to the event.

There are an unlimited number of events formatting the Universal Time chain called Eternity. Trying to analyze, find or create a new place for the small pieces, parts of the endless puzzle, is one of DVA's intentions.

Our culture forces us to frame art in styles: Modern Art, Impressionism, Naivism, Pop Art, Hip Hop, Classic, Sur, Minimalism and so on...

All of these have informational purposes only nowadays, as tags.

DVA uses them all, but it’'s not a decorative art. If you let the Universe pass through your transparency and project the output of the prism as an artwork, then join this field...
