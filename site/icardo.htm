Daniel Icardo

computer-drawn art

NOTES
Daniel Icardo, a French digital artist, attended the renowned Beaux Arts School in Paris. He is classically trained in art and remains influenced by Flemish and Italian masters, as well as Delacroix, Ingres, Corot and Sisley. "I believe the computer is a new approach in the history of art," he writes. "From the inner circle of painters to minimalists, the loop is done for painters using classical materials. Provided by computer, new materials afford a comeback to an intuitive approach..." Icardo uses Painter 4 and miscellaneous filters from Adobe. "Dotted wih good software he masters, the artist is reaching a level that old artists had with classical tools." (Translation from the French by Bernard Martoia)
