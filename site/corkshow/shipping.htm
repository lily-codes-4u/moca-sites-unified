MOCA: Museum of Computer Art
International Digital Art Show
Cork Gallery, Lincoln Center, New York City
January 3, 2004 through January 13, 2004 Shipping

Please ship art to:

Don Archer MOCA: Museum of Computer Art
515 East 19th Street
Brooklyn, NY 11226
USA

Please ship art as soon as possible . Art must be received by December 15.

Shipping charges prepaid by the artist.

Art may be shipped by US Mail, FedEx, United Parcel, international shipper, or hand-delivered (see below).

Art should be shipped properly packaged to resist transit damage.

There is a maximum frame size of 30" in the longest direction (width or height) for any submitted art.

All accepted art must be received ready-to-hang. Artists are strongly advised to present their art in the best fashion possible, which in most cases would include matting and framing behind glass or clear acrylic within a suitable frame. MOCA will do no framing, matting or presentation work beyond installing the art in the gallery.

Art will not be accepted after December 15. Artists located outside the continental US should especially anticipate delivery times and arrange to get their art delivered by the December 15th deadline.

Artists located within the NYC metropolitan area may personally deliver art to the receiving address indicated above, prior to December 15. Please click contact to advise and arrange for personal or hand-delivery of art.

Art that remains unsold at close of show will be returned to the artist, shipping charges collect or prepaid by the artist.

Fees must be received by December 15.
