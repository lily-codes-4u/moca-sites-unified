Image upload page

moca home contest info contact fee view images

Upload images from this page. You may submit one, two or three images. Still images in JPG format only.

Uploaded images are posted to our contest site for online viewing usually within 24-48 hours of receipt.
Images are posted chronologically as received.

Please insert your name in the file name to make it easier for us to associate the file with you.
For instance, FireEngine.jpg might be sent as DoeJohn_FireEngine.jpg.

Upload size may not exceed 200 kilobytes for any one image.
Images greater than 200KB will be broken in transit and will not be viewable here. Please check kilobyte sizes before uploading.

Images are subject to resizing. We will generally resize larger images to about 540 pixels wide or high. Artists are encouraged to upload images consistent with this parameter.

Images smaller than 480 pixels in longest dimension may not be competitive.

NOTE
Fees are $25.00 per image, payable upon upload. Please make payment via our fee page.

Use browse button to find image files on your computer.
