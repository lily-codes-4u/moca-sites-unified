Born in Madrid in 1974 this artist studied design at 'Infographics Studies Centre' in Madrid before training in painting at the 'Acro Verde' Artistic Centre.

A self-proclaimed visual artist, Velasco's work is inspired by the Pop-Art of the 1960's, as well as the combined cultural influences of technology, capitalism, fashion, and mass consumption.
