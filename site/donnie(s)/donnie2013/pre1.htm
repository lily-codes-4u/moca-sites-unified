The Donnie 2013

moca home view catalogs

Opens soon! Anticipate it!

The Donnie 2013 is our thirteenth annual digital art contest. It is the most prestigious digital art competition on the Web. It will open for entries January 2, 2013.

It is international in scope and open to all digital artists and photographers. Maximum six entries per artist. Still images only. JPG format. Image size approximately 1 - 2 megabytes each. Images posted chronologically as received.

It offers eight awards: first, second and third prizes plus five honorable mentions. Winning images celebrated in an online MOCA exhibit through the end of year 2013.

Contest judge is JD Jarvis, distinguished digital art critic and writer, co-author of "Going Digital: The Practice and Vision of Digital Artists".

MOCA will publish a full-color catalog of the show. To browse or order among our 17 published catalogs, click catalogs.

MOCA is a non-profit educational institution supported almost entirely by the contributions of artists. A modest fee per image will be required for entry.

Stay tuned! More information will be available on the MOCA site as the contest approaches.
