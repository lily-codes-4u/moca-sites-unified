Don Archer's Fractal Art

Best of the U-draw fractals, part 1

Here are the best of the first U-draw fractals drawn by me in response to the input of viewers, based on a two-function Mandelbrot formula written by Jonathan Osuch called Mandel(fn||fn). They were winnowed down by me from a group of some 200 submitted by viewers. The characteristic Mandel spiral is a motif of many of them, although I often have to explore deeply for interesting characteristics and contrasts, and find the justifying color scheme. Fractal art is truly the art of discovery, and U-draws are a collaboration between viewers and me in that discovery.

Thanks for the input!
