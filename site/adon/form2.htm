U-draw input form

Yes, you can create your own fractal by a simple input of
random numbers and functions and see your image displayed on this site.

I will draw a 256-color fractal for you in the Fractint engine
with a modificatiom of a formula called Tim's Error,
written by Tim Wegner and modified by me.

You won't know what your fractal will look like until you see it,
but neither do I. No fractal artist knows what his image will look like
until it is actually drawn on screen. You may very well surprise
yourself at the beauty of your image, even though your input is random.

The copyright on all images remains with Don Archer.

Please input three numerical values and up to four functions

INPUT VALUES
Input any value from -2 to +2. Whole numbers or decimal numbers
to two places are permitted. You may use such values as
-2, -1.27, -1, 0, .05, 1.75, 2, etc.
