Guest Gallery · Albert Juergens · Dream with a Rose

(1 of 7)

MOCAccess membership required to view this full exhibit

Albert Juergens, like Lewis Carroll, the famous author of Alice in Wonderland before him, has made a specialty of photographing girls, and seven of Juergen's portraits of girls are included here. Juergens lives near Windham, NY, where he operates an art gallery called Phantasma. He has long been a distinguished photographer. His manipulations (in Photoshop) are modest and subtle. The subject always takes precedence, but a careful study of the image will often reveal a background studiously elaborated and colors carefully shifted.
