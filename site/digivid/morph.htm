Digital Video and Audio Art · Morph · by Milán Kopasz

Digital Video and Audio Art
Morph
by Milán Kopasz
Flash Animation

Open external media

Milán Kopasz is 24 years old. He writes, "I finished my studies at Krea Contemporary Art School in Budapest in 2009 as a graphic designer. In the same year I started to study animation at Moholy-Nagy University of Art and Design in Budapest. I had some individual and collective exhibitions with my works. I took part in Good 50x70 poster contest in 2007, where my poster was shortlisted. In 2010 the same poster was selected for the '2010 Peace Calendar'. Now I'm focusing more on animation.

"This was a school project. The task was to make an animation with morph technique. And the film doesn't have to tell an exact story but express emotions and feelings. Actually it is a hand-drawn short movie animated frame by frame."
