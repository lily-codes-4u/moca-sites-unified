DONNIE 2020 FULL INFORMATION

moca home upload your art view all entered images pay by credit card pay by paypal

The Donnie 2020 is our 20th consecutive annual digital art contest and exhibit. It is international in scope and open to all digital artists, including photographers, beginner or advanced. Test your mettle. Compete with the best. Give new life to your art.

The Donnies are one of the most prestigious and influential digital art competitions on the Web.

Entries accepted thru January 31, 2020. You can submit your art today.

FEES
Payment accepted by credit card or PayPal. Enter your art early in January for maximum savings.

Fee is only $59.00 per image, if entered January 11 thru January 20.
Fee is only $69.00 per image, if entered January 21 thru January 31.

Maximum six entries per artist. Still images only. JPG format. Image size 1 - 2 megabytes each. Images are submittted via email attachment.

Contest offers eight awards: first, second and third prizes plus five honorable mentions and 12 director's choices, all given year-long exposure on the MOCA site.

LIVE GALLERY EXHIBIT IN CATSKILL NY
MOCA has arranged for the Atelier Progressif Creative Gallery in Catskill NY (USA) for a live print (physical) exhibit of winning and director's choice images from this contest. MOCA will print and hang these images March 15 - 28 at no charge to the artists. Click for details and full information about this LIVE exhibit at: Atelier Progressif Creative Space.

MOCA is a non-profit educational and charitable organization, chartered by the New York State Department of Education (USA). It is supported by contest fees and the contributions of artists, viewers, and friends. It has been the Web's primary showcase for digital art for more than 20 years.

Contest judge is Steve Soper. Steve has been a MOCA graphics editor for many years and has had a long and distinguished career as a photographer, digital artist, graphic designer, and Photoshop specialist. He is responsible for creating the Donnie catalog.

MOCA will publish a full-color catalog of the contest (at extra charge). To browse or order among our 25 published catalogs, click CATALOGS.
