(AI) Artificial Intelligence Galleries and Slideshows

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a collection of algorithmic and mathematical systems using complex programming and the distributed
power of the Cloud to mimic human intelligence. There is nothing mystical about it. Web programmers have relied on it to build
digital art programs that (astonishingly) convert text to art, and I have used such programs as Dall-E, Midjourney, and Wombo Dream
for just this purpose, with a light reliance on one or two graphic programs to adjust color and size if warranted. It has been said that
AI is already sentient (i.e., has achieved human feelings or emotions), but that is nonsense. I don't know what the future will bring but
right now the humanity of AI art is just a wishful dream.

Nonetheless, this is a labor of love. Each of these galleries or slideshows is more-or-less a work in progress. Please visit frequently to view
changes, deletions, and new art. I am having fun and maybe even creating digital fine art. This is a break-through technology, IMHO

Don Archer
Director
