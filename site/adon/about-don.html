About Don Archer

Don Archer founded the Museum of Computer Art and made fractal and computer art from 1993 on. These pages come from his own copies of his websites: an interview, his fractal music, the front page of donarcher.com and the U-DRAW fractals visitors could send as e-postcards. His galleries are in Don Archer's collection.
