Dan McCormack

Composer, artist, poet. Member of the Union of Composers of the USSR. Member of the International Writers' Union.
Participant of the Chelsea International Fine Arts Competition - Chelsea, Manhattan, New York.
Publications in the almanac "Russian Bell", "Sovremennik", in the publishing houses "Soviet Composer", "Music",
"Soviet Russia - Moscow, Amazon, Barnes&Noble Press, buecher.de (bucher.de), Lulu Press.
Nominee of the National Literary Award "Poet of the Year - 2018" - Russia. Winner of the International London Literary Prize, 2019.
Nominee of the Sergei Yesenin Literary Prize "My Russia", 2021. Awarded the medal "Sergei Yesenin 125 years" for his contribution
to the development of modern Russian literature, 2021. Over the years, exhibitions have been held at MOCA, the Museum of Computer Art,
dedicated to the works of outstanding digital artists - NY, USA. Individual paintings were purchased by private collectors in the USA, Germany, Israel.

Description:

Water hangs shyly in the middle of the path,
like a girl's color.
