DONNIE 2024 INFORMATION

This contest is now open for entries. You can submit your art today. Entries accepted thru January, 2024.

The Donnie 2024 is our 25th consecutive annual digital art contest. It is international in scope and open to all digital artists, beginner or advanced, including photographers. Artificial Intelligence art accepted! Test your mettle. Compete with the best. The Donnies are the Web's most elite and influential digital art contests. Placement in contest lends extraordinary prestige to entered artists, especially to prizewinners.

Fee is just $99.00 per image. Payment via PayPal or credit card.

To enter the contest click links top of page to submit your art and to make payment. These are two separate steps.

Images are posted to the contest site in order received. Submit your art soon as possible for top positioning.

Maximum six entries per artist. Still images only. JPG format. Image size 1 - 2 megabytes each. Images are submitted via email attachment.

Contest offers eight awards: first, second and third prizes plus five honorable mentions and up to 5 director's choices. Winning images are awarded year-long exposure on the MOCA site.

Contest judge is Don Acher, MOCA director and longtime digital artist.
