Guest Gallery · Solis · Farm Bottles

(1 of 3)

Solis is a New Zealand artist who started dabbling with an image editor - Ulead PhotoImpact - some years ago and has never looked back. Today he also uses Bryce and 3D Max, along with Photoshop. He has had no formal art training. He writes, "Although my real passion lies with the abstract form, I have branched into many other areas on my never-ending journey through this medium. For a while now I have been into the 3D artworld with a vengeance...It is rare for me to plan an image in its initial state. I leave myself as open as possible to the moment that surfaces onto my personal canvas. Silence tells me all I need to know." His images largely skirt the cliches of 3D art. His best work may be images like "Farm Bottles", appearing on this page, which renders a traditional still life without posture or pretense.
