Larry Carlson

computer-drawn art

NOTES
Larry Carlson grew up in New Jersey and currently sells his digital print art on the streets of New York City, often in front of the Museum of Modern Art. He has been making art since childhood. He writes, "What really keeps me pumping out so much new work is that digital image-making is a whole new way of creating art, it's a new game to play, a new frontier to explore. There has not been much great work done with it yet so there is no one's example to follow. I must forge my own path inventing it as I journey on...merging my dreams into the machine...I'm always careful that my work does not become a mere exercise in skill or just showing off computer special effects... the artist must take it further, harvest the power of the computer to create original expressions. To quote George Lucas, 'Special effects are nothing without a story line...' The computer is just a tool like a pencil or a paintbrush, what really counts is how much RAM of passion and megs of talent are in the artist." Carlson works in Photoshop, Bryce and other graphic programs.
