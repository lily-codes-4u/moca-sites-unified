As a special incentive to participating artists, the Museum of Computer art has arranged with the Atelier Progressif Creative Gallery in Catskill NY for a live (physical) print exhibit of 20 selected images from this contest. This print exhibit will include all winning images and director's choices from the contest. These images are printed in full color by the Museum of Computer Art at no charge to the artists and hung in the Atelier Progressif Creative Space.

Please see time and date of show and opening reception below.

ATELIER PROGRESSIF MISSION STATEMENT

"Atelier Progressif Creative Space in Catskill, NY, pushes the boundaries of digital art and technology. The gallery solicits critical essays, film, artwork, design, and process pieces that employ the tools of digital culture. We believe the new generation of artists has a unique perspective as it engages digital culture socially, professionally, creatively, and theoretically. Young thinkers and creators fuel the process of digitization and the subsequent culture erupting from these processes, and many of their decisions will impact the future of our creative perspective."

Atelier Progressif was founded and is directed by Yechiam Gal, an Israel-born artist, animator and curator. He teaches video art at Pratt Institute in Brooklyn NY.
