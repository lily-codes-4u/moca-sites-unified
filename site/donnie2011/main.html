Donnie 2011

moca home entries brooklyn gallery payment upload ship your art

First Prize $1000.00

The Donnie 2011 is our 11th annual international digital art contest and exhibit, one of the most celebrated and successful digital art events on the Web.

It provides opportunity for artists worldwide to participate in a prestgious event, to advance their careers, to expose their art to a sophisticated New York audience, and to compete for best-in-show $1000.00 cash prize.

Your art submitted by digital file (maximum five images). We then print your art professionally on 16" x 12" gloss paper with archival inks on a state-of-the-art HP printer, then frame your art in a contemporary glass-protected wood frame.

Art is hung in our Brooklyn gallery for the length of the show. An online exhibit on the MOCA site of all entered art accompanies the physical exhibit.

Entry fee is $125.00 per image. Maximum five images. Payment by credit card or PayPal. Please click payment link.

Art may also be provided pre-printed by the artist (framed or unframed) and shipped to the gallery ready-to-hang. Same fee applies. Click Ship your art for more information.

Eight awards are offered: first, second and third prizes and five honorable mentions. In addition to the first prize money award, winners get a prominent winners' page and exposure of their art online through the end of 2011.

A panel of digital art experts will serve as jurors for the event.

MOCA will publish a full-color catalog of the show as part of our series of elegant catalogs documenting the history of digital art in our era.

All art will be offered for sale. Artists will advise sale price of their art. MOCA takes no sales commission. Prints are the property of the artist and if unsold will be shipped upon request to artist at artist's expense.

Digital images uploaded here should generally be about 1 to 2 MBs in transmission size.

You can make payment before or after upload via payment link top of page. Payment assures acceptance into the exhibit.

You can submit your art (1 - 5 images) today!
