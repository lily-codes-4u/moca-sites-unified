artificial intelligence art

This section complements the main MOCA collection.
It serves primarily as the showcase of the Artificial Intelligence Art
of Don Archer in his exploration of this new and daring venue.
Clicking links on this page accesses all art on this site
as well as on the site at no charge.

OUR POLICY
No advertising. No Facebook or Meta. No Twitter or X. No distractions.
Dedicated to the online exhibit of digital fine art in all its forms and manifestations.
