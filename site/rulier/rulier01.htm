Portraits in Depth · Jean-Marc Rulier · Audra

(1 of 12)

This exhibit has been updated with two new images on December 1, 2006.

Jean-Marc Rulier, born in 1964, is a French senior design engineer. He writes, "I create my images from photos that I postwork using Photoshop and 3DSMax...as a former professional photographer, I am much concerned with angles and light issues." He uses a powerful texture effect to lend depth to the flat screen image. He tries "to bring up an atmosphere" and "to find beauty within the beast." He wants you "to feel even if it hurts." These are women of a strange, uncommon beauty.
