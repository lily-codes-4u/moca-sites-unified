Lyndsey Warren

I love all forms of the creative process: art, photography, poetry, music, moments, and more. I hold a BFA from the Hartford Art School where I studied childrens' book and digital illustration in addition to photography and art history.

I enjoy creating in both traditional mediums and materials as well as digital mediums. I am also a digital artist who specializes in digital illustration, retouching, photomanipulation, and digital mixed media.

I love merging my digital art skills with my photography and traditional fine art to create photo-based digital fine artworks. It is my goal for others who view my work to recognize and appreciate digital art as a true fine art making method. We are living in the next great art movement and it is time people realize just how powerful a tool technology is in creating artwork.
