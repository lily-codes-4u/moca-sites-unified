The Donnie2008 Contest

moca home upload contact fee view contest

This is our eighth annual online contest, one of the largest and most prestigious digital art contests on the web.

This is a competition and exhibit of quality digital art. It is open to any and all digital artists.
Artists may enter and participate in the contest today.

Winners secure long-term exposure on our site and the prestige and honor accruing to Donnie winners. All images published in an online MOCA gallery during the course of the contest and winning images published in an online Donnie2008 winners gallery through December 31, 2008.

There is a $25.00 exhibit fee

per image. Maximum three images per artist.

Payment must accompany submission. All payments made via our fee page.

Images accepted through Wednesday, February 20, 2008, 11:59 p.m. (ET)

Awards will be announced on or about March 1, 2008.
First, second and third prize winners and five honorable mentions will be selected. This contest is juried.

Upload size may not exceed 300 kilobytes for any one image. Images greater than 300KB are aborted in transmission and cannot be viewed here. Please confirm kilobyte size in Photoshop or other graphic program before uploading.

Images should be about 600 pixels in width but not over 540 pixels in height. While all images are subject to re-sizing, our exhibit pages accommodate this size best. It will give your images the strongest presence on our pages.

Images appear on the contest site usually within 24 to 36 hours of receipt.

JPG format only!

Click upload to submit your art to the contest.
