Donnie 2011

Ship your own printed art framed or unframed.

You can send your own art to the Donnie 2011 printed on paper or canvas, ready for us to tack to the gallery walls. Or send framed, ready-for-hanging. Art may be any square or rectangular size but may not exceed 42 inches in width or height.

Maximum five prints. Regular $125.00 fee per image applies, payable by payment link top of page.

Art should be suitable tubed or packaged and shipped prepaid to:

Museum of Computer Art
Att: Don Archer
139 11th Street
Brooklyn, New York 11215
USA
Tel: 718 788 1313

Please ship your art so it arrives no later than January 20, 2011. A prepaid return shipping ticket should accompany the art to facilitate its return. We assume no shipping charges either receiving or sending art.

Click upload to submit your images digitally to the Donnie 2011 online exhibit and for our catalog. Upload size in JPG format should be about 1 - 2 MBs.
