Guest Gallery · Luis Miguel Caamaño · CocaCola 1

(1 of 7)

Luis Miguel Caamaño is from Columbia, South America. He writes, "I found in art my way to express what's inside me...I love photography but I have not taken any class so pretty much I'm just a big fan of it. I use Photoshop to place what the camera can't--what I feel--so that's why my pics are kind of abstract although there's a lot of figurative shapes. I also write and I try to challenge myself to think how could I make art not just a "thing" to see but a Way to Live. In my blog I write that sometimes we decided to live a lie and that everything we do is based on trying to keep that lie safe but that's not the real life...and through art I think we can find the way of ending that vicious circle and finilly find what we really are....part of a huge piece of art that is creation and mankind."

Of the seven images included in this exhibit, four are from the artist's "CocaCola" series and three are from his "Bogota Mujer" series. Each of these series applies a different technique and sensibility.
