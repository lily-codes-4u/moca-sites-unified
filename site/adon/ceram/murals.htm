Don Archer digital ceramic art

Wall mural of water lilies composed of 84 tiles each 6" square. Overall size 8' wide x 2' high.

Digital murals bring a fresh, dramatic and sometimes a post-modern look to contemporary interiors. These murals are created from a high resolution digital image which is transformed in the computer to a pattern of multiple images. These images are then computer-processed onto tiles for installation to stunning effect in restaurants, bars, clubs, lobbies, hotel rooms, kitchens, baths, swimming pools, or wherever. Photographs or digital images may be derived from any source. The water lily mural on this page was processed from a photograph taken by the artist at the Brooklyn (NY) Botanical Garden.

Murals are always custom. They are generally priced uninstalled at approximately $150.00 per square foot, depending on individual tile size and other factors. The mural shown on this page (16 sq.ft.) was priced at $2400.00 uninstalled.

Murals may require close collaboration between artist and client.
