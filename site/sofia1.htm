The art of Lorraine Sofia

the art of Lorraine Sofia

CUBIC SERIES I

COMMENTARY AND BIOGRAPHICAL NOTES
Lorraine Sofia grew up in Westchester County, New York, and is a graduate of the Parsons School of Design and New York University. She has been an artist in oils and acrylics for over 40 years, and has exhibited her work in New York, Palm Beach, Westchester Co. and Monterey, Ca. She lived in Florida for 19 years and currently makes her home in Daly City, Ca. Conventionally trained in the arts, she was inspired by a fractal calendar to try her hand at computer art, starting years ago on an Amiga. She is self-taught on the computer, and moves freely now among Fractal Design Painter, Kai's Power Tools, Bryce, CorelDRAW, "and anything else" to help create her designs. Her favorite art is the illuminated manuscripts of France, Italy and the Netherlands from the period 1300-1500. Her computer art has a rich fractal aspect and is characterized by the use of a vivid palette integrated with black and white.
