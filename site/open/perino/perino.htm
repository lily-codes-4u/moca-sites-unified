"Eliana Perino, born Genoa, Italy, 1957, deals with iconic languages and art in the dialectic from 1981 as teacher and former student, and she is organizing and conducting courses on art education and art teaching for adults. The expression of creativity, as a need to catch internal vibrations, is a personal outcome through the use of video and photo codes. In the last few years, details and shadows found their best expression in the computer art.

"Photography assumes the role of the picture, a picture that first is imagined, evocated, looked  at with the eyes and the heart; when caught, it becomes space to draw thoughts and vibrations. Details of reality become other than themselves, and shape and colour become plots to tell about the passion and the sense, and in the men's word 'lacerations'".

Eliana Perino's website
