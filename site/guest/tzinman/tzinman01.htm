Max Tzinman writes, "With my images I try to transform urban and natural landscapes, with their intrinsic, and sometimes contradictory values, to render a parallel, deeper reality. I am not a photographer or painter but an image storyteller. I am not trying to 'freeze the moment', but to render a continuously changing world. I am trying to push the viewer to think and feel: 'what comes after?', 'where is this going?', and sometimes even: 'how did we get here?' Something is happening both before, and after that captured moment...

"I am building a parallel reality that moves, breathes, and lives as much as the everyday reality we know and experience."
