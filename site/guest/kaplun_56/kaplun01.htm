Guest Gallery · Pavel Kaplun - Image Manipulation · ...auf den schönen warmen Abend!

(1 of 7)

Pavel Kaplun was born 1965 in Kusnezk, Russia, where he graduated as a chemical engineer. He now lives in Hanover, Germany, where he works as a programmer and graphic and product designer. He lectures on Photoshop, Flash and other graphic applications. He was a winner of a special Photoshop 2003 award. His images are notable for their clarity, wit and humor, despite a kind of surrealism that is embedded in his work. But there's no morbidity or darkness here, only fun.
