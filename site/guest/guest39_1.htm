Guest Gallery · Terry Calen - 3D Rendered Art · 2001_06

Seven images selected here are from Terry Calen's year 2001 and 2002 collections. He uses an Apple Macintosh computer and 3D rendering software from Electric Image, and post-processes his images for color, texture and shading with other tools. He is 56 and lives in Burien, Washington, near Seattle. He writes, "I have worked in the digital imaging industry for 13 years and in the photographic imaging industry for 15 years before that. For as long as I can remember, I have been motivated to make pictures. I was a photographer for many years, before discovering computers and 3d rendering technology in the late 1980s. At about that same time, I became involved in the growing digital imaging industry in Seattle. Long hours and other, personal distractions helped to defocus my creative work. I dabbled in commercial art and interactive media until 1998 when I began again to think seriously about exploring my creativity. I view my work as exploratory and evolutionary. Each image is a series of visual discoveries that build on and reinforce one other. In turn, each image builds on previous images as my experience and vision evolve. One of my major objectives is to explore creativity; to learn to see differently."
