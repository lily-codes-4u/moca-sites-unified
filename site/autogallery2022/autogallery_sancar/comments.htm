Sibel Sancar

She is a computer software programmer (Industrial Engineer) and painter.
She began painting in 1990, and still continues her studies today in her home studio.
She paints with watercolor & acrylic media and is also a digital artist.
She has had six solo and various group exhibitions
She has been working on digital art for more than ten years.
