don lebow

don lebow

fractal art

Biographical notes
Spells his name in lower case, "an affectation, probably," he writes. Makes his home in Lahaina, Hawaii. Born and raised in the Pacific Northwest. Graduated in 1971 from Whitman College in Walla Walla, WA, "with no interest in either computers or art." Unmarried. Tends to stay up too late at night, "a victim of the dreaded just-one-more-tweak syndrome." "In real life," works in retail. "In virtual life," is an irredeemable online computer addict and longtime sysop on CompuServe's AtariGaming forum.

Commentary
Uses the Stone Soup Group's Fractint to create his fractals. "For me the process is akin to a hunt. Since there are so many variations on any one formula, variations on plotting, variations on color, it's easy to spend hours tracking down that one combination that makes me sit up and say, 'That's it!' Serendipity plays as large a part as anything else. I am not so adept that I have any real idea of what a given change might yield. The buzz when something hits is great...I enjoy discovering images that don't especially look like what most folks think a fractal 'should' look like. A little shaking up of preconceptions (my own as well) is always a very healthy thing." His images are buttressed by an infallible and intuitive sense of color and proportion. "Deconstruction" is an unfractal-like contribution to the art.
