Bernice Greenberg

I attended Tyler School of Fine Art in Philadelphia, and graduated from Teachers College, Columbia University in 1944 with a BS in Art and Education.

My love of art began in early childhood and increased as the years went by.

For twenty five years I taught art in an elementary school from kindergarten to sixth grade. During that time I pursued my desire to paint and exhibit. My work has been exhibited in galleries in NYC, and a museum on Long Island and it hangs on the walls of numerous clients.

I never stopped studying. I have had shows featuring cloisonne jewelry I designed and fabricated as well as etchings, sculpture and ceramics. I received an MS in Art and Education from Queens College, NY.

After retiring, I loved teaching children privately.

Five years ago I traded in my easel and canvas for a computer and Corel Painter for brushes, paints, and so much more. It has been a period of exploration, challenge and satisfaction. I am fortunate to have had the opportunity to be part of the digital age.
