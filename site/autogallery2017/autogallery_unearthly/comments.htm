Unearthly Demagogue

Master Unearthly Demagogue is a veteran visual art shaman, psychedelic visionary and poet-philosopher from Israel. Having been born and raised in the Soviet Union helped his lifelong indoctrination into the vast sea of cultural and spiritual education, in the best tradition of past European masters and outsider art/art brut luminaries.

His trademark creative adventures blend these passions in fascinating and unique ways. This artist's humble life's work gives wings to his infinite psychedelic imagination thanks to his signature mixed-media techniques of translating his visions into the canvas using custom digital processing techniques of his traditionally drawn images. This ever-expanding alternative universe explores outlandish mental landscapes, quirky existential commentary and unorthodox humor, all the while diving ever deeper into the profound realms of consciousness and psychonautics.

Last but not least, the artist's original style, genuinely experimental attitude and life philosophy are matched by a genuine personality and open horizons to engage in prolific dialogues and mentoring for the younger generation which is important to him. The artist often accents how important it is to remain down to earth, be socially involved in the community and serve as a paragon both morally and ethically, side by side with one's dynamic creative development.
