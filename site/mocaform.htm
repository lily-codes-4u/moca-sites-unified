THE MOCA NETWORK APPLICATION

Join the MOCA network of distinguised art sites.
MOCA is one of the most heavily-trafficked, comprehensive, frequently-updated and respected computer art museums on the Web.

MOCA is non-profit, has no advertising, and has no agenda except the promotion of computer art.

MOCA welcomes membership to its network of distinguished art sites.

Members are required to post a MOCA link to their site. There is no other obligation except to continue to operate a quality site. We will provide instructions on installing the link to your site.

MOCA links back to all member sites via its MOCANET page.

The MOCA network is intended to bring traffic and recognition to member sites, and to bring MOCA to the attention of an even wider audience via member site links.

MOCANET is exclusively devoted to personal art web sites. It excludes commercial sites, sites that represent a collection or group of artists, sites that are primarily sales-driven, sites where the art is not a major feature, or sites carrying a distracting burden of advertising. Sites should be user-friendly, quick and easy to access, and fully functional.

Please be aware that not all sites are accepted into the MOCA network. Upon receipt of this form, MOCA will evaluate your site for membership.
