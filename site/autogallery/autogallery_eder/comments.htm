Donna Eder

Donna Eder is an internationally published visual artist compositing nature and the nuances of computer technology. She has special media interest that includes the integration of technology, art, and digital photography. With passion, she creatively applies techno-artistic expression into new possibilities.

Her Eco-Green Line includes Techno Sculptures and techno-wearable necklaces. She enthusiastically recycles computer circuit boards, device pieces, and other technology discards into beautiful sculptures, reviving life from the recycling piles. The necklaces demonstrate the crafting of handmade techno-wearable pieces combining soft yarns, fabrics, and computer components/ for medallions. These Donglers (Donna's Danglers) are one-of-a-kind necklaces.

Mrs. Eder's digital renderings are sensational sellers as magazine covers. Some include The Journal of Rehabilitation and Journal of Occupational Therapy.

Vocationally, Mrs. Eder promotes artistic applications through supporting artists in web assistance and development, as well as teaching special needs and underserved populations.

Academically trained at Columbia University, New York, Mrs Eder holds a Master of Arts in Computers in Education that includes programming graphics, graphic tablet drawings, storyboards, and software design. Other studies include computer graphics at the City Colleges of Chicago, Ray Vogue College of Design-Chicago, and State University of New York. Additionally, she holds current computer certificates in A+, Net+, and MOUS.
