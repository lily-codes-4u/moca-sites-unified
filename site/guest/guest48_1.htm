Guest Gallery · Dorothee Holtz - Abstractions · Afternoon Walk

Dorothee Holtz is a German artist living near Frankfurt/Main. She has an M.A. in ethnology and oriental languages and PhD. in education. She has worked for 15 years in the marketing deptartment of an international company, where she developed skills in presentation and image manipulation hardware and software. She now uses Picture Publisher, Photoshop Elements and Corel Photo Paint for her art. She writes, "...my favorite style is abstract painting - with intense colors, lights, and structures to gain depth effect. It is my intension to explore the tension between chaos and harmony, distortion and balance, confusion and the healing of emotions." She is inspired by Bauhaus art and such artists as Picasso, Miro, van Gogh, Monet, Kandinsky, Nolde, and August Macke, who travelled in Northern Africa, she tells us, to study desert and mediterranean colors. The geometric patterns and the color harmony of oriental rugs are also an inspiration to her, along with the Chinese 5-elements philosophy of energy and color interaction. Her hobbies are playing the guitar, singing French chansons and Tai Chi. She is represented in MOCA's guest gallery with four of her abstractions.
