Guest Gallery · Swami · CamDiaz

(1 of 3)

Swami, 47, lives in Peoria, Illinois, where he works part-time on small commercial projects such as logo-design, fliers and posters. He writes, "When I was 31 working as an illustrator drawing clip-art, it was announced that an intern from Bradley University was going to teach us how to "draw" on the computer. In 1979 I received a degree in fine art from Southern Illinois University. It was bad enough that I now had to make a living drawing cartoon birdies, pilgims, bunnies and, of course, Santa Claus, now we had to learn to use a Macintosh computer (or 'The Magic Box', as some of us referred to it). 'Hey...I can't even type!', I grumbled. This was 1987." His real name is Jim Wombacher. He calls his art VectoPop. It is drawn in Illustrator and Photoshop and tries to combine his "traditional art training with the new electronic technology."
