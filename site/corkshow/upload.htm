Upload images from this page. You may submit one, two or three images.

There is no fee due or payable for uploading images. The fee applies only for art accepted and included in the physical show.

This is a juried and curated show. Uploaded images will be screened for inclusion in the Cork Gallery exhibit. You will be advised promptly by email if your physical art has been accepted for inclusion in show.

All art included in the physical show will also be included in an online virtual MOCA exhibit that will accompany the Cork show.
