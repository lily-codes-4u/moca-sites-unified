The Donnie 2015

Opens for entries Friday, January 2, 2015 at 12:01 a.m ET.

The Donnie 2015 is our fifteenth annual digital art contest. It is the most prestigious and influential digital art competition on the Web.

Fee is only $45.00 per image (same as last year). Payment via PayPal, bank transfer, or other means.

It is international in scope and open to all digital artists and photographers. Maximum six entries per artist. Still images only. JPG format. Image size 1 - 2 megabytes each. Images are downloaded by the artist via our upload page and posted chronologically to the contest site as received.

It offers eight awards: first, second and third prizes plus five honorable mentions. Winning images are given year-long exposure on the MOCA site. A catalog of the show will be available.

Contest judge is JD Jarvis, distinguished digital art critic and writer, co-author of "Going Digital: The Practice and Vision of Digital Artists".

MOCA will publish a full-color catalog of the show. To browse or order among our 21 published catalogs, click catalogs.

Contest will close for entries Saturday, January 31, 2015, 11:59 p.m. ET.

MOCA is a non-profit educational and charitable organization. It us supported exclusively by contest fees and the contributions of artists, viewers, and friends. Thank you for visiting our site and participating in our activities.
