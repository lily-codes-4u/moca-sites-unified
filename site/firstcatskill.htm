First Catskill Computer Art Show

The Atelier Progressif Presents

The First Catskill Digital Art Show

in collaboration with the Museum of Computer Art
Curated by Don Archer, MOCA director

The Exhibition will showcase digital art by talented MOCA artists from all over the world who have contributed their work for the show. It is a reservoir of what is the most original and dramatic work created on the computer. It includes 3-D rendered art, fractals, enhanced photography, animation, mixed media, and computer-drawn art.

At Atelier Progressif Creative Art Space
75 Bridge Street, Catskill, N.Y. 12414
March 11, 2016 - April 8, 2016
Opening reception Friday, March 11, 2016 from 6 p.m - 8:30 p.m.

Gallery hours:
Thursday thru Sunday
Noon to 4:30 p.m., and by appointment

The show is a reflection of our mission to seek out and identify individuals who utilize technology to create and promote meaningful new artwork. We want to be part of shaping how art is created and consumed in the digital era.

Atelier Progressif Creative Art Space is looking to give space and support to artists who use digital tools to create art and art projects that benefit society.
