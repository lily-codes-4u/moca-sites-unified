MOCA: Museum of Computer Art Digital Print Show 2004
Cork Gallery, Lincoln Center, New York City
January 2, 2004 through January 13, 2004 Main

THANK YOU, DIGITAL ARTISTS!
THIS SHOW HAS BEEN OVERSUBSCRIBED.
NEW SUBMISSIONS ARE NOT BEING ACCEPTED.

MOCA is proud to announce an exhibit, competition and sale of print art at the prestigious Cork Gallery at Lincoln Center, 65th Street and Broadway, New York City.

A reception will be held at the gallery on Sunday, January 4, 4:30 to 6:30 p.m. Everybody welcome.

See note at the bottom of this page for continuation of the show at another venue.

This is a live group show sponsored and arranged by MOCA: Museum of Computer Art through the courtesy of Cork Gallery and the Visual Arts League (VAL). These are all not-for-profit organizations.

There is a maximum frame size of 30" in the longest direction (width or height) for any submitted art.

There is an administrative fee of $90.00 for each print or other outputted art included in show, exclusive of shipping charges. Click fees for details.

This is a juried and curated show. First prize (best-in-show), second prize and third prize awards will be offered.

All accepted art must be received ready-to-hang. Artists are strongly advised to present their art in the best fashion possible, which in most cases would include matting and framing behind glass or clear acrylic within a suitable frame. MOCA will do no framing, matting or presentation work beyond installing the art in the gallery.

Artists will be asked to advise a selling price for their art. The gallery will be open to the public at no charge and a large and enthusiastic audience is expected. MOCA will take a 25% commission on the sale of all art, and the artist is advised to include this amount in the selling price.

See our shipping page for shipping instructions and receiving address. All art must be received here in NY by December 15.

This show is curated by Steve Soper:
steve@freetransform.com

Don Archer, MOCA director
hello@museumofcomputerart.org

NOTE

MOCA is glad to advise that it has arranged for the Cork Gallery exhibit to be extended for another two weeks at a venue in New Jersey after its closing on January 13th in New York. This will give additional exposure to the art. This is an academic venue under the auspices of the Visual Arts League of New Jersey (a not-for-profit community arts corporation). This NJ exhibit will be located at:

Old Student Lounge
Robert Wood Johnson Medical School
University of Medicine and Dentistry of New Jersey
675 West Hoes Lane
Piscataway, NJ 08854

Dates of this NJ show:
January 15th through January 31, 2004.
There are no additional artists' fee required. All art included in the Cork Gallery show will be included in the NJ show, unless permission is refused by the artist.
